DeskCat
About Download

A cat that lives on your desktop.

DeskCat is a small black cat in a cardboard box with its name on the side. It sits in a corner of your screen, watches your pointer, and every now and then hops out to explore.

What it does

  • Wiggle the pointer near the boxIt rises up, paws on the rim, and bats at you if you get close.
  • Hover gently over its headThat's petting. Happy eyes, purring, little hearts.
  • Click itIt crouches and jumps out of the box.
  • Move slowly along the floorSometimes it stalks, wiggles its bum and pounces. Sometimes it just watches.
  • Swipe fast right past itIt startles and hops away, or ducks into the box.
  • Drag itYou pick it up by the scruff. Drop it back in the box.
  • Throw its toyIt chases it, bats it about, pounces on it, and trots off with it in its mouth.
  • Hold the toy up highIt climbs up after it, one paw over the other.
  • Spin the pointer in circlesIt gets dizzy. Swirly eyes, little stars.
  • Leave it aloneIt grooms, yawns, stretches, loafs, and falls asleep. Eventually it goes home.

Make it yours

You can rename it, pick its coat and eye colour, change the box colour, choose its toy, make it bigger or smaller, and decide how playful it is. It remembers everything.

Small and quiet

  • No accounts, no internet, no tracking. It just runs on your Mac.
  • Drawn entirely in code, with no images. It slows itself down while it naps, to go easy on your battery.

Installing it

DeskCat needs macOS 13 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). Download it, unzip, and drag it into Applications.

Because it isn't signed by a paid Apple developer account, your Mac will block it the first time. Try to open it, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
